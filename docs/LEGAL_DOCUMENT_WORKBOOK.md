# Legal Documents Workbook

## Important boundary

The files in `docs/legal/` are structured drafts, not legal advice and not approved public legal notices. They intentionally contain organization fields that must be completed from real information, then reviewed by qualified counsel and the organization owner.

Do not publish them on the public website, represent them as final, or use them for real patient operations until that review is complete.

## Documents prepared now

| Draft | What it is for | Must be completed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privacy policy | Public website data practices, forms, cookies, and contact route | Owner and counsel |
| Terms of use | Conditions for using the public site and portal | Owner and counsel |
| Notice of privacy practices | HIPAA-facing patient notice, if applicable | Privacy officer and counsel |
| Security and privacy notice | Plain-language explanation of technical safeguards without compliance claims | Owner and counsel |

## Information to collect before review

1. Legal organization name and business address.
2. Privacy officer, security officer, and contact methods.
3. Actual service area, phone number, and approved website email.
4. Whether and how HIPAA applies to the organization.
5. The actual categories of data collected through public forms and portals.
6. Approved vendors and contractual status, including any needed business associate agreements.
7. Retention schedule, patient rights process, complaint process, and effective date.
